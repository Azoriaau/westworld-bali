Villa Pasir is designed for slow mornings and easy evenings. Living, dining and kitchen open fully onto the pool deck through wide timber doors, so the whole villa breathes with the sea breeze.

Both bedrooms are true suites, each with a walk-in robe and a stone-lined bathroom that opens to a private garden shower. Natural materials run throughout: honed terrazzo, recycled ironwood and hand-woven linen, chosen to age gracefully in the tropical climate.

Set on a quiet lane off Jalan Petitenget, you are a short walk from the beach, and a few minutes from Seminyak's best restaurants and boutiques. It is equally suited to a lock-up-and-leave holiday home or a high-yield rental.
