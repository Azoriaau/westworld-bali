Villa Angin is the largest and most private residence in the collection. Set behind a hand-laid stone wall, it unfolds around a 14-metre pool and a mature tropical garden, with an open-air bale for morning yoga or long lunches in the shade.

Four ensuite bedrooms are split across two wings, giving families and groups room to spread out. The main suite opens to its own garden terrace and a stone bathroom with a freestanding bath under a timber skylight.

A broad living pavilion with a pitched alang-alang-style roof keeps the interior cool and bright, while the entertainer's kitchen flows straight out to an outdoor grill and dining terrace.
