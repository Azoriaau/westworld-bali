Villa Laut is the heart of the collection: a generous three-bedroom home arranged around a 12-metre infinity-edge pool. A double-height living pavilion, lined in recycled ironwood, frames the garden on one side and the pool on the other.

Upstairs, a rooftop terrace catches the late-afternoon breeze and golden sunsets over the Indian Ocean. It is the kind of space where a quick drink becomes the whole evening.

Every bedroom has its own ensuite, and a separate media room can double as a fourth sleeping space for visiting family. Behind the scenes, staff quarters, a full laundry and a butler's pantry make it just as easy to live in as to rent out.
